The repository is organized as a multi-process system: `backend/apps/api` exposes REST/WebSocket endpoints while `backend/apps/engine` runs feed ingestion, VEE evaluation, and schedulers; both share `backend/libs/shared` modules (kernel, config, redis, auth, kyc, plans, market, trading, challenge, reward) and communicate via Redis Pub/Sub with single-writer-per-account locks. The `frontend/trader` Next.js app proxies `/api/*` to the backend in dev and ships static pages in production. `deploy/` composes the two backend processes behind Nginx with TLS on Ubuntu, mounting persistent volumes for KYC data and Let's Encrypt certs. `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs typecheck and tests across both backend and frontend, and `docs/` documents each phase P0–P6 plus the SRS and frontend design.
